# SampleShot — Support

**Screenshots for sound.** Tap once, capture whatever your iPhone is playing at full quality, and file it into a local sample library with folders, tags, chopping, stem separation, and a full editor.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a capture?
Tap the record button in SampleShot (or use the widget / Control Center control), then confirm **Start Broadcast** in the system sheet. iOS requires this confirmation for any app that records system audio — the same flow as screen recording. A red indicator shows while capture is running.

### Why does my recording of Apple Music come out silent?
DRM-protected audio (such as Apple Music / FairPlay content) is muted by iOS in system-audio capture. Most other sources — YouTube, Safari, games, voice memos, most streaming apps — capture fine.

### Can SampleShot record phone or FaceTime calls?
No. iOS excludes call audio from system-audio capture entirely, for every app.

### Where are my recordings stored?
Everything lives on your device in SampleShot's local library. Nothing is uploaded to us — we don't run any servers or accounts. You can export any sample or chop via the share sheet.

### What does SampleShot Pro include?
Unlimited stem separation, the mastering suite, Mic In field sampling, unlimited layers and sections in the sequencers, and an unlimited chop library. Capture, chopping, FX, and export stay free forever. Pro is a subscription managed through your App Store account — cancel anytime in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

### How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?
Open SampleShot → Settings → **Restore Purchases** while signed into the same Apple Account.
