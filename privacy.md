# SampleShot Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 10, 2026**

SampleShot is built local-first. Your recordings, samples, chops, and projects are stored only on your device. We operate no servers, require no account, and collect no personal information.

## What stays on your device

- All audio you capture or import, and everything you make from it
- Your library organization (folders, playlists, tags), edits, and settings
- Your trial state and purchase state (verified through Apple's StoreKit)

None of this is transmitted to us. We have no access to it.

## Network features you can trigger

Some optional features contact external services **only when you use them**:

- **Song identification.** When you ask SampleShot to identify a song, a short audio fingerprint or snippet of the selected recording is sent to Apple's ShazamKit service and/or the AudD music-recognition API (audd.io) to find a match. The audio is used for recognition and is not linked to your identity by SampleShot. See the [AudD privacy policy](https://audd.io/privacy/) and [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **Song metadata and lyrics lookup.** Matched songs may be enriched via the iTunes Search API and public lyrics services using only the song's title/artist — no audio and no personal data are sent.

## Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of that data is described in [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Analytics, ads, tracking

None. SampleShot contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and does not track you across apps or websites.

## Children

SampleShot is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
